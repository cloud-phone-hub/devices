[    0.247434][    T1] init: [libfs_mgr] __mount(source=/dev/block/dm-5,target=/odm,type=erofs)=0: Success
[    0.265829][    T1] init: [libfs_mgr] __mount(source=/dev/block/vdc,target=/mnt/vendorpatch,type=ext4)=0: Success
